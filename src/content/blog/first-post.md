## Why Visit Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is a tropical paradise known for its diverse landscapes, ranging from lush rainforests and arid plains to highlands and sandy beaches. It is also famous for its ancient Buddhist ruins, including the 5th-century citadel Sigiriya, with its palace and frescoes.

- **Wildlife:** Spot leopards in Yala National Park.
- **Beaches:** Relax on the golden shores of Mirissa.
- **Culture:** Explore the sacred city of Kandy.
